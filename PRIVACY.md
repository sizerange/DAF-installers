# Dual Apartment Finder mobile privacy policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Dual Apartment Finder (DAF) is published by Marja Mustonen under the Sizerange name. Contact sizerange@gmail.com for support or privacy requests.

## Information on your device

The native app saves browsing preferences, hidden or saved apartment choices, and a cached copy of the public apartment shortlist on your device. These records support your browsing experience and offline access. Removing the app removes its locally stored data, subject to your device backup settings.

## Network requests

The app connects to the DAF service to retrieve public apartment information and images. Requests necessarily expose technical information such as your IP address, requested resource, request time, and device or network information to the hosting and image providers. Operational logs may be retained by those providers for service reliability, troubleshooting, and security. The native app does not require an account or send your local saved choices as an account profile.

If a build enables optional AI recommendations and you request them, relevant apartment and search information is sent to the DAF service for processing. Recommendations are advisory. The standard browsing app does not automatically run paid AI requests.

## Permissions and advertising

The native app does not request your phone contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location. It does not include advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

## External websites

Original listing links and the Economy link open websites in your system browser. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Economy uses separate website authentication for private financial planning. The native app does not obtain that website's login credentials or private financial records.

## Providers and retention

DAF uses Render for hosting and third-party listing or image providers for the apartment information you choose to view. Public listing records are retained independently of your local device preferences. Device preferences and cached results remain until cleared or the app is removed. Hosting providers retain operational records according to their service policies. We do not sell information submitted through the native app.

## Requests and changes

Email sizerange@gmail.com to request help or exercise applicable privacy rights. Include a description of the request, and avoid sending passwords or private financial records. This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change.
